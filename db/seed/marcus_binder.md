## About Marcus

Marcus is 34 years old and has lived at Maple House since 2016. He communicates with single words, gestures, and a small set of picture cards kept in the kitchen drawer. He understands far more than he says, speak to him normally, not slowly.

Marcus is easy-going most of the day but can become overwhelmed quickly in loud or crowded places. When that happens he covers his ears and looks for the door. Do not crowd him; give him space and somewhere quiet.

## Communication

- Uses single words: "coffee", "bus", "no", "Lisa", "help".
- Points, nods, and shakes his head reliably. A thumbs-up means he is genuinely pleased.
- Picture cards: coffee, bowling, bus, music, quiet room. Cards are worn; replacements needed.
- If he repeats a word twice ("bus, bus"), it is urgent to him. Respond right away even if the answer is "later".

## Daily routine

- Wakes around 7:00. **Hot drink with breakfast at about 7:30, his order is a coffee, double cream double sugar ("double-double") from Tim Hortons** when there's a trip out; instant coffee at home otherwise.
- Day program Monday to Friday, picked up at 8:45.
- **Saturday afternoon is bowling.** This is the anchor of his week. He lines up his shoes by the door from about noon.
- Listens to music in his room most evenings, over-ear headphones.
- Lights out around 10:00.

## Likes

- The Toronto Raptors. Watches every televised game. Keeps a Raptors cap on the bedpost.
- Bowling (see Saturdays). Likes to keep his own score sheet even though he doesn't write the numbers.
- Bus rides, window seat, left side.
- His sister Lisa, see contacts.

## Dislikes / what upsets him

- Loud, echoing rooms: food courts, gyms, school assemblies. He will cover his ears, then try to leave.
- Being rushed through breakfast.
- Last-minute changes to Saturday plans.

## What helps when he is upset

1. Move somewhere quieter, or step outside.
2. Offer the headphones.
3. Don't talk a lot. One short sentence, then quiet.
4. When he is calm, offer a choice of two things to do next, he settles faster when he picks.
